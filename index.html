Know a link before you click it.

Paste any URL. URLyze breaks it apart, checks it for common phishing red flags, and gives it a clear risk score. Runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

    // HOW IT WORKS

    Three steps, zero guesswork.

    [ 01 / 03 ]

    Parse

    The URL is split into scheme, host, subdomains, domain, port, path, query and fragment.

    [ 02 / 03 ]

    Check

    Each part is tested against known phishing patterns and weighted by how risky it is.

    [ 03 / 03 ]

    Score

    Weights are added up to a 0–100 score with a plain-language reason for every point.

    // CHECKS

    What we look for.

    No HTTPS +15

    Punycode (xn--) +20
